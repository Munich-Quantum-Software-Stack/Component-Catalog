The **MQSS CUDA-Q Adapter** serves as a bridge between the CUDA-Q programming interface and the
Munich Quantum Software Stack (MQSS). It enables seamless integration by providing two primary
access points: the Munich Quantum Portal (MQP), a cloud-based interface for remote quantum job
submission, and a high-performance computing (HPC) access point for users working on local or
institutional HPC resources.
